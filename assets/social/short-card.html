How a short works
Borrow it, sell it, buy it back cheaper. Your loss is capped at what you posted.
worked example · $HMM
150% margin · numbers illustrative
01
Post collateral
You put up ETH. Nothing else is accepted, and this is the most you can ever lose.
you post 4.410 ETH
margin 150%
02
Borrow and sell
The protocol takes tokens from the vault and sells them into that token's own pool, in the same transaction.
borrow 124,000 HMM
sold for +2.940 ETH
03
Hold the position
The sale proceeds stay inside the position as collateral. Interest accrues in ETH every block, so waiting is never free.
collateral 7.350 ETH
debt 124,000 HMM
health 2.08
04
Buy back and repay
You owe tokens, not dollars. Buy the same 124,000 back at whatever they cost now and hand them over.
owe 124,000 HMM
keep the difference
It falls 60%
+1.76 ETH
Those 124,000 tokens now cost 1.18 ETH instead of 2.94. You buy them back, repay the vault, and keep 1.76 ETH on 4.41 posted. A 40% return, less the ETH interest you paid to borrow.
It rises 108%
liquidated
The debt grows until it reaches your collateral. A keeper closes the position and takes an 8% bonus. You lose what you posted, and not a cent more. There is no margin call chasing you.
